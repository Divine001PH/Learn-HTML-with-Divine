EBA & EGUSI SOUP

Eba and Egusi Soup is a classic Nigerian dish made from smooth, firm garri (cassava) dough, served with a rich, savory soup prepared from ground melon seeds (egusi), palm oil, vegetables, peppers, crayfish, and assorted meat or fish. It is hearty, flavorful, and commonly eaten by hand.
Eba and Egusi Soup is one of the most popular traditional Nigerian meals. Eba is made from processed cassava (garri), while Egusi Soup is prepared mainly with ground melon seeds, palm oil, vegetables, pepper, and meat or fish.
The dish has deep roots in West African food culture, particularly among several Nigerian ethnic groups. Cassava became an important food crop in Nigeria after its introduction to West Africa, while the use of melon seeds in soups has long been part of indigenous cooking traditions.
Over time, different regions and families developed their own versions of Egusi Soup, using ingredients such as ugu, bitterleaf, okra, crayfish, stockfish, beef, goat meat, or assorted meat. Today, Eba and Egusi is enjoyed throughout Nigeria and has become one of the country's best-known dishes internationally.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups ground egusi (melon seeds)
  • 500 g beef — assorted meat works too
  • 200–300 g stockfish
  • 1–2 cups palm oil
  • 100–200 g dried/cooked fish (optional)
  • 1–2 cups palm oil
  • 1 large onion
  • 3–5 fresh peppers (scotch bonnet/ata rodo)
  • 2–3 tbsp ground crayfish
  • 2–3 tbsp ground dried pepper — adjust to taste
  • 2–3 cups ugu leaves, chopped
  • Spinach can substitute.
  • 1–2 stock cubes, optional
  • Salt to taste
  • Water/stock as needed

Steps to make

  1. Wash and season the meat with onion, salt, and seasoning cubes, then cook until tender.
  2. Wash and soften the stockfish, then remove the bones.
  3. Blend the fresh pepper and onion together.
  4. Heat palm oil in a pot over medium heat.
  5. Add chopped onion and fry briefly.
  6. Add the blended pepper and fry for about 5–10 minutes.
  7. Add the ground egusi and stir thoroughly.
  8. Cook the egusi for about 5–10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  9. Add the cooked meat, stockfish, dried fish, and meat stock.
  10. Add ground crayfish and additional pepper according to taste.
  11. Stir everything together and allow it to simmer for 10–15 minutes.
  12. Add the chopped ugu or spinach and stir.
  13. Cook for another 3–5 minutes.
  14. Taste and adjust the salt, pepper, seasoning, and palm oil as needed.
  15. Turn off the heat and serve hot with eba.
